# THE LOOP (round 12)

One draft to grow step by step. Sources: the round-12 research (RESEARCH.md 4a to 4e, hero H3) and the story bible (STORY.md) in the session scratchpad.

## Page order

The home (`/drafts/the-loop`, `Home.tsx`) is one short flow: eight chapters and the contact. Each chapter has one big point, one 3D moment and a link to a deeper page when one exists. The detail lives on the deeper pages.

0. Hero: the care calendar builds itself while three named agent cursors place its parts. The loop stands around the screen: on the left the test card (step 1, "appointments-calendar.spec.ts", "1 · written on the old app", the four scenario names of `testLines`), on the right the check receipt (step 3, "3 · automatic checks": typecheck, lint, test:unit, build and "same test, both apps", each "passed"), and the approval seal on the screen's corner (step 4, "4 · APPROVED BY A PERSON • MERGED •"). They replace the two pills "Checks passed" and "Approved". Wide landscape screens: the cards float at depth beside the screen, partly behind its edges; phones and portrait tablets: the two cards stand under the screen (two columns from 560 px), the seal sits on the screen's corner, and Replay stands beside the seal. The line "AI agents build inside tested rules. A person approves every change." One quiet role line under it: "Web, mobile and server apps, from Kosovo." The top bar keeps Work, CV and Email.
1. AI engineering, kept in control. Three proof lines. The new calendar screen comes apart into its four layers and closes (`Explode.tsx`). Link: "How the workflow works".
2. Design System v2. Text on the left (one line, 41 / 868 / 22); the seven boards on one large screen in the room on the right (`Boards.tsx`). Link: the Design System case.
3. Server work, a skill we grow. Three proof lines (Za!, 16 → 2, patient times) and the bug-hunt sentence. The 16 → 2 scene (`Queries.tsx`); under it, the live Za! table with two seats in a browser frame. Links: Play Za!, Za! on GitHub.
4. Apps for iPhone and Android: two real phone screens set down on the table (`PhoneFrame`). Link: the Bayyinah TV case.
5. Web3, Incentiv: the public sign-in screen arrives as a slab from the right.
6. Own projects: Offday is the big slab; six small tiles.
7. Range and fast learning: the breadth table and four dated "learns fast" lines. No 3D.
- The room from above (`Above.tsx`): every chapter's screen on the floor of the room, like a map. Each screen is a link back to its chapter; the last one opens the workflow page. Not in the room index.
8. Contact: the line, email, CV, GitHub. The room's light goes out.

The lamp (`LampMark.tsx`, `lamp.ts`): the room's light as a mark. A sphere of 25 × 25 amber dots, shaded as one light falls on it, with a tilted ring cut through it (the loop). It is in the top bar before the name (56 px; 44 px under 768 px) and, on screens 1280 px and wider, at the top of the room index (56 px).

The room index: the seven chapter names at the right edge on screens 1280 px and wider. The concept asked for six names; the new home has seven chapters, so it lists seven. At 1280 px and wider the home's content column is narrower by the index's width on each side, so the index never covers content.

Workflow page: `/drafts/the-loop/workflow` (`Workflow.tsx`). Head (Back to the loop, "How the workflow works", a short intro), the pinned four-step scene (`Steps.tsx`), the day dial (`Ring.tsx`), "Checks that caught real mistakes" (three cards), "For engineers" (stack, gates in two CI jobs, decision record titles, step guides by stage), then "Next case: Care platform".

Care case: `/drafts/the-loop/care-platform` (same chunk). It is the case template: full-bleed head with a large real screen, chapters "What it does", "The problem", "What the team built", "What changed", a ring on the proving part of each screen, the 16 → 2 figure, stack pills, "For engineers".

Design System v2 case: `/drafts/the-loop/design-system` (`DsCase.tsx`). Head: the seven boards in the same carousel as the home, full width. Chapters keep their text and have no figures: what a design system is, the problem, what the team built (five steps from research to merge), what changed (96.6 %). "What changed" has one figure row, as on the Bayyinah TV case: 96.6% large, with 41, 868 and 22 beside it (the home chapter's figures and labels). Linked from the home chapter "Design System v2".

Bayyinah TV case: `/drafts/the-loop/bayyinah-tv` (`BayyinahCase.tsx`). Head: the public library page. Chapters: what it does (series page, ring on an episode card), the problem (members on web, iPhone and Android, three ways to pay, Arabic; pricing page, ring on the plan card), what the team built (one web app, rebuilt from an empty template; the Arabic library beside two App Store images), what changed (34 pages; 270+, 2 languages, 3 ways to pay; store links). Linked from the Bayyinah TV card in "Apps for iPhone and Android".

Each case ends with "Next case": care → design system → Bayyinah TV → care. The workflow page ends with "Next case: Care platform". The links from the home carry `fromHome`, so "Back to the loop" goes back in history and the home restores its place. Shared case parts are in `CaseParts.tsx`; case styles are in `cases.css`.

## Look

- Ground `#0b0c0f`, cards `#15171c`. Ink `#f2f3f5`, `#b4b8c1`, `#959aa4` (all 6:1 or more on the ground and on cards). One accent: amber `#ffb547`. Green `#7ef0a6` and red `#ff8f8f` only for pass and fail.
- Public Sans (preloaded by index.html). First screen: two sizes, display 46 px and body 16 px. Gentrit Technical Mono 14 px only for real text from the repos, times and labels.
- The room (`room.css`, `room.ts`): one floor, one light. Every 3D section uses the same values. View: `perspective: 1800px`, `perspective-origin: 50% 40%` (`.room-view`). Rig: `.room-rig`, the only `preserve-3d` parent; no opacity below 1, overflow, clip-path, filter or mask on it. Floor: a still radial shadow 64 px tall under the slab (`.room-floor`). Light pool: one amber radial gradient at 0.11 behind the slab (`.room-light`), moved with transform only, 1400 ms. Sheen: a 45 % band, `rgb(255 214 150 / 0.2)` at its centre, from `translateX(-100%)` to `translateX(230%)` in 1100 ms, 200 ms after a slab lands; a warm band, because a white band does not show on the white screens. Arrival pose: `translate3d(0, 4 %, -360px) rotateX(10deg)` to `none` in 900 ms. Exit: `translate3d(0, -2 %, -120px)`, opacity 0, 220 ms. Card in front: from `translate3d(±80px, 12px, 180px) rotateY(∓16deg)`, 1000 ms. Percent moves are rounded to whole CSS pixels of the slab before a run. Every slab rests at `transform: none`.
- Case head ground: care `#0f1a2b`, Design System v2 `#191a33`, Bayyinah TV `#47262d`. On the last two, head text is `#e2dfe6` and labels `#c2bcc8` (7:1 or more).
