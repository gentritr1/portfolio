## Scope

Routed developer portfolio for hiring managers and senior engineers: a short phone-first scan, with deeper case-study reading. DESIGN.md records the built system; CONTENT.md and PRODUCT.md govern facts and provenance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm broadcast control room monitors the products Gentrit has shipped. Six domains are channels, live authored recreations are monitors, the project index is the schedule, and a case page is a channel tuned in.

OWN-WORLD: Matte graphite panels, restrained daylight, hairlines, Archivo display at 112% stretch, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body and Martian Mono labels. Six muted channel tints and one warm on-air/focus signal. Rectangular 44 px controls, small corner radii, monitor bezels. No neon, pills, glow baths or scrolling glass surfaces.

STORY: The visitor reads the name and role, changes the featured monitor, browses the full schedule, then tunes into a factual case study with milestones and a guided demonstration. Contact details and an owner-confirmed CV finish the site.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name, role and product claim on the left; a shallow six-pane Signal Stack on desktop. The featured channel selector and monitor follow. Phones hide the decorative hero stack and use an edge-to-edge fixed-height wall with accessible swipe and buttons.

FORM: Code-led broadcast master-control language. Static imagery is either a public page/store listing, an authored invented-data recreation, or a geometric sharing card with recorded provenance.

FINISH: Keep DESIGN.md and ASTRA-VERIFICATION.md current. Check 375, 820 and 1440 px in both themes and motion preferences, keyboard use, initial bundle cost, each lazy 3D chunk and layout stability. Hosting is a separate owner decision.
