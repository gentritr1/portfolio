# Diagnosis: why the loop sits at 52–59 and circles

Date: 2026-10-06. Written for the owner. Evidence: LOOP-BRIEF.md, ROUND-3-BRIEF.md, PORTFOLIO-RESEARCH.md, round-1 … round-9 reviews, the first-screen sheets (thumbs3 … thumbs9), the picker thumbnails of all 54 drafts, the draft stylesheets (`src/drafts/*/*.css`), `meta.json` of every draft, CREATIVE-CONSULT.md, ASTRA-CREATIVE-BRIEF.md, ASTRA-MOTION-CRAFT.md, the calibrated jury of the early creative drafts (`creative-first-five-calibration.json`), and the OFFBEAT and FORM captures in `public/personal/shots/`.

Short verdict first. The loop did not fail at craft. It failed at range. Nine rounds and 34 drafts explored one idea: a log of results, a frame that shows the proof, a hairline between them, on cream paper with one accent and a mono for the small print. The rubric rewards that idea, the shared briefs prescribe it, and the polish track protects it. The owner's own best visual work (OFFBEAT, FORM, LINJA, FJALËKRYQ, ISSUE) sits outside the loop and was never scored on the loop's bar. The scores cannot rise because the only two points that are not already 8–9 ("original" and "hooks") are the two points the rules forbid the builders to chase.

---

## 1. Why the scores sit at 52–59 and never near 70

### 1a. The rubric and how reviewers use it

**70 is not a reachable target on the reviewer's own scale.** Every review states the scale: 7 = a good personal site, 8 = distinctive, 9 = best in class. 70 means 10 on all seven points. 63 means 9 on all seven, which is "best in class" on seven independent axes at the same time. No reference site the owner named would score 63 on this bar: designeer.xyz would lose on "shows actions and seniority", rauno.me on "straight to the point", Bruno Simon on "not overwhelming". The target was set above the scale's ceiling, so every round reads as a failure even when the page improves.

**The seven points pull against each other.** Points 2 (not overwhelming), 4 (meaningful motion only) and 3 (harmony) are satisfied by removing things. Points 6 (original) and 7 (hooks) are satisfied by adding one thing nobody else has. Under the rule list the builders could only remove. The arithmetic shows it: projector's best round is 9 + 8 + 9 + 8 + 9 + 7 + 8 = 58. Points 1, 3 and 5 are already at 9. To reach 63 it needs original 9 and hooks 9 with calm still at 8. That page cannot exist under the current rules, because a 9 on original needs a move the slop list or the motion canon forbids.

**"Original" is anchored at 7 and is scored relative to the loop, not relative to the web.** The numbers: across 34 loop drafts and 9 rounds, "original" was 7 in most of them, 8 in six (sampled-ground, redline, sampled-review, sampled-frame, two-readers ×2), never 9, and below 6 only in round 1 and round 2 (orbit-index 4, strike-index 5, cited-claims 5, tenant-switch 5, proof-tiles 6). The round-4 review has a section titled "Why original is stuck at 7". The round-6 review gives sampled-review an 8 and then says: "It is a combination of two round-5 frames, so it is not 9." That is the tell: the reviewer measures novelty against the other drafts in the loop. A page can be new inside this loop and still be a scrollytelling layout the world has seen (round-7 devil: "Sticky media frame beside a scrolling text column … known on sight"). Original 9 was never defined, so no reviewer gave it.

**Hooks and motion are scored from PNG captures.** The rule since round 1 is "capture one mid-animation frame for every claimed motion". A frame proves that a thing moved. It cannot prove that a thing feels good. So the reviewers can only reward motion that is legible in a still: a strike, a hairline, a number that writes in. Motion with mass, momentum, interruption or sound (ASTRA-MOTION-CRAFT §2) is not scorable with this evidence rule, so no builder made any. Point 4 settled at 8 for 30 drafts on the same three devices.

**The reviewers are fresh agents, not independent judges.** Each critic reads the same LOOP-BRIEF, the same rule list and the same prior reviews. They inherit the loop's taste. The self-scores and the review scores converged: round 1 the gap on "original" was 1.5–2 points; by round 6 "self-scores are within 2 points of the review on every draft"; by round 9 the critic's total is within 1–3 of the builder's. Agreement is not accuracy. A jury that agrees with the builders after six rounds has learned the builders, and the builders have learned the jury.

**Each round's new rule list caps the next round.** Count the rules a builder must satisfy now: 7 bar points, 6 rules after round 1, 12 rules copied from round 2, 6 new in round 3, the scroll canon S1–S6 with durations and curves, the big-card sizes to the pixel, 5 chrome and state rules, the craft bar (six widths, CLS 0, 44 px, nothing on hover), 15 copy rules, a blocked-word list of about 30 words, 21 display rules, "no dark ground", "one accent", "two faces", "no instruction text", "no name above 10 %", "no two drafts open on the same screen". About 100 rules. Every one was written against a defect. None was written toward a quality ("the type must be beautiful", "the colour must be chosen, not safe"). A builder who satisfies 100 negative rules produces the one page that breaks none of them. That page is projector.

### 1b. The process

**Convergence started after ten drafts.** The owner's convergence goal is dated round 3: "Combine the best parts of all directions into drafts that aim for 63+. From round 4, the top two drafts stay and get polished." At that point the loop had seen four round-1 drafts and six round-2 drafts, all text-device ideas (citation, strike, log, margin note, switcher). The "best parts" that were combined were parts of one idea. Round 3's six "directions" are four combinations and two "bold new" ones, and all six open on the same care plate (round-3 review: "All six opened on the care plate; five were pale").

**Polish yields +1 and the reviewers said so three times.** Round 4: "Polish will not close that gap … the gap to 60 is the 'original' point on all three, which polish cannot move." Round 5: "A polish pass yields +1, never the +2 the §6 rule wants." Round 6: "Six polish passes across five drafts: five gave +1, one gave 0, none gave +2 … Polish will not close that gap." The loop then ran three more polish rounds (7, 8, 9) on projector: 55 → 56 → 56 → 57 → 58. Four points in five rounds, all of them in craft, none in original. The process optimised a local maximum with high precision.

**One creative director wrote every brief, to the pixel.** ROUND-3-BRIEF.md gives each of six directions its layout in ASCII, its card size (1200 × 720, 880 × 560), its radius (12 px), its elevation rule, its hover state, its durations, its easing, its palette with hex values and contrast ratios, and its typeface pair with file names and weights. PORTFOLIO-RESEARCH §4 does the same for rounds 8 and 9. The builders had no design decision left. Six builders with one brief each from one author produce one author's taste six times. That is why the thumbs8 and thumbs9 sheets read as one site in six colours.

**The "radical alternative" is never built.** The devil's advocate was added in round 8 and did its job: round-8 devil, projector: "Drop the log. Show three exhibits only, each a full-width real screen." Round-9 devil, projector: "two halves of equal weight. The log, numerals and tray go away." Round-9 devil, personal-studio: "An exhibition page: each own project gets one full viewport with one live control (the drum grid plays; the trefoil turns)." The round process says "Polish: the builders fix what the critic and the devil's advocate proved." A radical alternative is not a proven defect, so it is dropped every round.

**The facts and the NDA push the work toward numerals.** The strongest results (16 → 2, 805 tokens, 36 components, route-by-route parity) are invisible: no public screen shows them. So drafts draw them as squares, bars and 160 px digits (then-now, rebuilt-twice, projector row 03). Round-8 devil: "Three drafts open on a database fix, drawn as squares and not as a screen … to a peer, 16 queries → 2 is an N+1 fix: good work, but mid-level and backend." Meanwhile the public, visual, 3D work (OFFBEAT, FORM, FJALË, Morse Trainer, Za!) has no NDA and sits at the bottom of every page at 0.42–0.61 scale (round-9 critic: "they look like thumbnails of someone else's site").

**The early drafts were never scored on the loop's bar.** The 18 pre-loop drafts (Crafted, Fun, Experimental, Professional) were judged on design / usability / creativity / content. The calibrated creativity jury (same 7 = good, 8 = distinctive, 9 = SOTD scale) gave aisle 8.6, fjalekryq 8.4, diff 8.2, linja 8.0, bitrate 8.0. The loop's "original" never passed 8. The loop opened with round 1 as if those drafts did not exist and never compared against them. The one review that did look across both sets, CREATIVE-CONSULT §2, named the keepers: issue (type), swiss (letter tiles), riso (print artefact), savefile (all-28 grid), wall (band composition), dither (shader). None entered the loop.

### 1c. The design quality

Where the work is mediocre, with evidence.

**Typography.** 30 of 34 loop drafts pair a text face with a mono for ids, years, roles and results. The mono is doing the same job on every page: it says "technical" and "small print". Display type exists on five pages only (year-stack 160 px numerals, both-stores 96 px claim, rebuilt-twice 72 px Fraunces, then-now Anton figures, projector outlined numerals). No loop draft has a typographic signature a visitor would name. The pairs cycle through the same pool of ten files: Bricolage + JetBrains (pinned-decisions, strike-index), Bricolage + Plex (sampled-ground, sampled-review), Public Sans + a mono (cited-claims, decision-record, projector, sampled-frame), Libre Franklin + a mono (orbit-index, redline), Archivo + Martian (both-stores, proof-tiles), Newsreader + a mono (changelog, release-brief, added-clauses, two-readers), Literata + a mono (specimen, statement-of-record). Nothing is set with optical size, tracking or measure as a decision; the round-3 brief sets sizes and nothing else. Compare the early set: issue sets "Shipped." at about 300 px in a serif and the page is the type; swiss re-sets letter tiles on scroll; linja draws its own 5×7 glyphs in discs; zine cuts Anton into crossing bands. Those are type decisions. The loop's are type defaults.

**Palette.** After round 1 the rule was "light grounds". Since then 14 of 32 drafts sit on a near-white paper (#F2F2EE, #F3EEE3, #F7F6F1, #FBFBF9, #F5F4F0, #F6F6F4, #F3F4F0, #FAFAFA, #EEF0F2, #FFF8F0, #F6F5F0, #F3EFE6, #ECECE8, #FBF3E6) with one accent. The committed fields are few and three of them are the same move: projector and sampled-frame on brat chartreuse #D9F26B (round-7 devil: "memorable for one week, then it dates the page"), then-now's blue half, token-source's blue field. The sampled-colour drafts (sampled-ground, sampled-review, sampled-frame) hand the colour decision to a formula at fixed lightness and chroma; the result is a row of muddy maroons and pinks (thumbs5, Viva Fresh card). The "no dark" rule also removed the one ground on which the owner's strongest assets look right: OFFBEAT and FORM are black with hot orange and copper. On cream paper they read as foreign objects.

**Composition.** At 1440 nearly every loop first screen is: identity sentence top-left, a column of text at the left, one plate at the right. The thumbs3, thumbs8 and thumbs9 sheets prove it: six screens, one wireframe. Round-8 devil: "Five drafts, one message … The owner does not choose between five pages. The owner chooses between five themes for one page." The early set has six distinct compositions in its first six thumbnails (a disc board, a card table, a crossword, a dense ledger, a magazine cover, a shelf).

**Motion.** Three devices, 34 drafts: strike-and-write (brief, release-brief, strike-index, statement-of-record, year-stack, rebuilt-twice, added-clauses, two-readers, then-now), hairline-to-pixel (margin-notes, pinned-decisions, year-stack, proven-cv, redline, sampled-review, sampled-ground, projector, sampled-frame, token-source, both-stores, two-readers, then-now), plate cross-fade on the current row (projector, sampled-frame, two-readers, then-now, tenant-switch, proven-cv, personal-studio). Durations 160–280 ms, ease-out. Nothing has mass, momentum, a preview state, a spring or an interruption. The reviewers themselves counted the result: "five marks for one state" (round-7 devil), "four signals for this row is current" (round-9 devil). ASTRA-MOTION-CRAFT's eight rules (objects with states, momentum hand-off, blur with position, one playful spring per draft) appear in none of the loop drafts.

**3D.** Zero. No loop draft has a canvas, a shader or a WebGL context. The early set had linja (OGL instanced discs, 7k instances in one draw call), lap (MSDF melting names, Kosovo sun), facing-pages (page-curl shader), orbit (refraction), and `/drafts/lab` with 17 working moves (CSS3D matrix, screenshot-lit scene, fluid solver, solar light, MSDF, curl). The only "3D" in rounds 1–9 is a screenshot of OFFBEAT or FORM. Round-9 critic on projector: "OFFBEAT and FORM: the whole home page at 880 css px from a 2880 px source, 0.61 scale … the nav and controls are about 8 px."

**Imagery.** Four of five round-8 drafts opened on the Bayyinah TV pricing page; the round-9 devil found it still in three of five first viewports. Store frames are shown at 1.45–2.0× on a 2× screen (soft) on every round-9 page. Round 3 opened all six drafts on the care recreation. The pages show the same four pictures in nine different frames.

- **Layout families:** 7 across 34 drafts, but the spread is 11 L+F, 9 S+C, 3 SCR, 7 IDX, 1 GRID, 1 PAIR, 1 ROOMS. L+F and S+C are the same page with the frame either fixed or stacked: both are "words at the left, proof at the right, the current row decides the proof". Together they are 20 of 34. - **Motion devices:** 3 account for 29 of 34 drafts. Strike-and-write 9, hairline-to-pixel 13, cross-fade-on-current 7 (with overlap). The other devices are a FLIP reorder, a fold, a count, a theme flip, a wipe and a spread.

### 2.3 Compared with the early bands

The 18 pre-loop drafts have 13 layout families in 18 drafts (a disc board, a hover racer, a riso poster with a tray, a seam with two skins, a crossword, an HLS player, a card table, a magazine spread, a dense ledger with 28 live cells, a book cover with Arabic facing text, a supermarket shelf with a receipt, crossing type bands, an image wall, a Bayer-dithered hero, letter tiles, an orb, a TV, a low-poly desk). **Plain answer: yes, the loop converged too early, on the wrong axis.** It converged at round 3 on "the log and the frame" after ten drafts of one family, and then spent six rounds and 24 drafts making that page correct. It converged on craft (which it won: CLS 0, 44 px targets, fonts under 300 ms, reduced motion with 0 differing pixels) and never on identity. The best page in the loop is a very well-made page that any strong design engineer could have built for any strong developer. Nothing on it is his.

## 3. Typography, colour and 3D: the specific weaknesses, and what wonderful looks like for this person

### Who this person is, in the material

Web + mobile + full stack, five years, Kosovo. Two platform rewrites. One React Native codebase in two stores, three times. English/Arabic right-to-left, four-language care UI, Albanian apps. And on his own time he builds things that *work*: an eight-step Web Audio drum machine with swing and a WAV export; three mathematical sculptures rendered live in WebGL with copper, chrome and porcelain materials and a "type a word, cast a sculpture" studio with no runtime dependencies; a Morse trainer with Farnsworth timing; an Albanian daily word game with a 21k-word dictionary; a server-authoritative multiplayer card game. His signature is not "results". It is "it runs, in the browser, and you can touch it". The loop never used that signature once.

### Typography

Weak now: a grotesk or a reading serif plus a mono, at body sizes, on every page. The mono says "engineer" and nothing else. The display sizes that exist are numerals (160 px "2026", 96 px "16 → 2"), which are a trope in the Awwwards studio set (round-7 devil). Nothing is tracked, no optical size is used as a decision, no face is pushed to the end of its axis.

Wonderful for him: one face used with commitment, at one extreme, as the picture. The repo has the material: Gentrit Display (Hubot Sans) with `wdth` 80–120 and `wght` 200–900 is a variable display face that can go from condensed 900 to extended 200 on one axis; nobody in the loop used the axis (lab move 12 drives it from scroll velocity and sits unused). Fraunces with `opsz` 144, `SOFT` 100 and `WONK` 1 is a true display optical size; the loop used it at 72 px once. Anton is a poster face; then-now used it for two numbers. Amiri is a real Arabic text face; only facing-pages used it. Jersey 10 (a bitmap face) exists in `public/fonts`; only savefile used it. The 5×7 disc glyphs in linja are the only drawn letters in the repo. Wonderful means: pick one of these and set the whole page in it, including the metadata, the way mek.gallery sets everything in pixel type and offgrid sets everything in its own sans. The mono is allowed once, for one line, not as the voice of the small print.

### Colour

Weak now: cream paper plus one accent in 14 drafts; a brat-green field in the two leaders; sampled hues that come out as dull maroons; no dark ground since round 2. The one-accent rule was written against "dark + one neon accent" slop. It then banned every committed two- or three-colour identity (the early set had mustard + blue + black on fjalëkryq, safety yellow on black on linja, oxblood + cream + gold on facing-pages, felt red + cream + six suit inks on deal, fluo pink + blue on riso). Those are palettes. "Paper + cobalt" is a default.

Wonderful for him: a palette that is a decision and comes from his own material. OFFBEAT is black #0E0E0E, hot orange #FF5A36, acid #D9F26B, bone #F2EDE4. FORM is black, copper #B5652A, chrome, porcelain, and the page accent changes with the material (his own idea, already shipped). Viva Fresh is Albanian-supermarket red and green. Kosovo has a real sky at a real hour (lab move 14). Any of these gives three colours with a reason. Dark must be allowed again where the material is dark; the rule should be "no dark ground with a lone neon accent and nothing else", not "no dark ground".

### 3D and motion

Weak now: no 3D; three motion devices; motion judged from stills; the owner's WebGL shown as 8 px thumbnails. The one place a draft touches his 3D (projector rows 10–11) it rings a screenshot of a speaker.

Wonderful for him: his own renderers, live, on the home page. FORM has no runtime dependencies, so its trefoil (a parametric curve, copper material, inertia on drag, material swap that re-tints the page) can be ported into a draft inside the 150 kB budget. OFFBEAT's drum grid is Web Audio and DOM; it runs anywhere. The lab already has the screenshot-lit scene (move 1), CSS3D real DOM in perspective (move 2), the disc board (move 3), granular sound (move 4), Morse timing (move 5), verlet letters (move 6), MSDF (move 7), the curl (move 8), the solar light (move 14). That is not "generic 3D". That is his portfolio of 3D, and the honest claim "everything on this page runs" is one no reference site makes. Motion that belongs to him: a sculpture that turns with inertia and stops; a drum step that lights on the beat; a letter that drops into a crossword cell with a two-bounce settle; a disc that flips with a click. Each of those passes the owner's own bar 4 (it explains or gives feedback) better than a hairline does, because the motion *is* the proof of the skill.

## 4. The plan

### 4.1 Change the rubric

1. **Retire 70 and 63 as targets.** Keep the seven points for the record, but score only four: 1 straight to the point, 5 actions and seniority, 6 original, 7 hooks. Convert 2 (not overwhelming), 3 (harmony) and 4 (meaningful motion) into the craft gate: pass or fail, with the existing craft bar. They are already 8–9 everywhere; scoring them hides the gap. Target: 34 of 40, with original and hooks at 9 each.
2. **Define original 9 and hooks 9 in words a reviewer can test.** Original 9: a visitor who knows rauno.me, designeer.xyz, offgrid.inc, bruno-simon.com and the 2025–26 Awwwards portfolio winners cannot name a site this page copies, and the page's rule could not exist for another developer (CREATIVE-CONSULT M4 and M9). Hooks 9: a visitor would describe one specific moment to a friend in one sentence. The reviewer must write that sentence. If they cannot, it is 7.
3. **Score original against the web, not the loop.** The reviewer names three real sites the draft beats on that point and one it loses to. "A combination of two round-5 frames" is not a reason to withhold 9.
4. **Score hooks and motion from a live session, not from PNGs.** The reviewer drives the page in the browser pane, scrubs the signature moment at 10 % (ASTRA-MOTION-CRAFT §4 check list), and records a 10-second screen capture for the artifact. Mid-frames stay as proof that motion exists; they stop being the proof that it is good.
5. **Add one scored point: "type and colour as a designer judges them."** 7 = correct defaults, 8 = a decision a designer would notice, 9 = a palette or a setting someone would screenshot for a mood board. This is the point the owner is actually unhappy about and the rubric does not have.
6. **Calibrate the jury before each round on an anchor set.** Score linja, aisle, issue, fjalëkryq and projector on the same bar first, blind, so the scale has anchors above 7. A critic who gives linja original 7 is not calibrated.
7. **Self-scores stay in meta.json but leave the review table.** Builders do not learn the jury that way.
8. **Require a spread.** If three drafts in one round land within 1 point on original, the critic ranks them and writes what separates them, as the early creative jury did.

### 4.2 Change the process

1. **Round 10 is a divergence round.** No shared layout brief. No scroll canon. No big-card spec. No "light ground", no "one accent", no "two faces", no "no name above 10 %". Six builders, six one-page briefs (§4.4), each with its own rule, type, palette, hook and one moment. The only shared text is the keep list (§4.3).
